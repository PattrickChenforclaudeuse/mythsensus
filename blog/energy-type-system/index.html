The Five Energy Types: Which One Are You?

Cosmic Library · 6 min read · Mythsensus Editorial

Each person is born with a distinct energetic type — a fundamental pattern in how they generate, exchange, and deplete energy. Knowing yours changes how you work, decide, and rest.

✦ Mythsensus articles are for educational and self-reflection purposes only. All interpretations are symbolic in nature and do not constitute medical, psychological, financial, or life advice.

Five Ways of Being in the World

The Energy Type System categorizes individuals into five fundamental types based on their energetic constitution at birth. Each type has a defined Strategy — the optimal way of navigating decisions and initiating action — and an Authority, the inner guidance system that, when followed, leads to correct choices for that type.

⚡
Generator · ~37% of population
Consistent, renewable life force energy. The Generator's strategy is to respond — to wait for something in the environment to respond to, rather than initiating from scratch. When aligned, Generator energy is sustainable and deeply satisfying. When out of alignment, it burns out or feels trapped.
⚡✦
Manifesting Generator · ~33% of population
A hybrid type with Generator energy plus a direct connection to the manifest world. Faster-moving than pure Generators, often multi-passionate, and capable of skipping steps others cannot. Still benefits from the Generator strategy of responding, with added capacity to inform before acting.
◎
Projector · ~21% of population
Not a consistently generating type — Projectors are here to guide, not to work in the traditional sense. Their strategy is to wait for the invitation before entering major life areas. Projectors see deeply into others' energy and processes; when recognized and invited, this becomes their greatest gift.
→
Manifestor · ~8% of population
The only type designed to initiate. Manifestors carry closed, repelling energy that can create resistance when others don't understand what they're doing. The Manifestor strategy is to inform before acting — not to ask permission, but to neutralize the impact of their independent movement.
◑
Reflector · ~1% of population
Completely open energetically — Reflectors sample and amplify the energies around them. They are the rarest type and the most sensitive to environment. A Reflector's strategy is to wait a full lunar cycle (28 days) before making major decisions, giving the Moon time to move through all gates.

Strategy and Authority: The Inner Compass

Knowing your type gives you the outer framework. Your Authority — the specific decision-making center that is consistent and defined in your chart — gives you the inner compass. There are seven Authority types: Sacral, Emotional/Solar Plexus, Splenic, Ego/Heart, Self-Projected, Mental/Environmental, and Lunar.

"Most people make decisions from the mind — from what they think they should want, what others expect, or what seems logical. Authority bypasses all of this and points to the part of the body that actually knows."

In the Multi-System Context

Within the Mythsensus framework, your Energy Type and Authority contribute to understanding how you are best designed to move through the world — independent of timing. Where Western astrology and BaZi reveal what themes are active, your Energy Type reveals the ideal mode of engagement with those themes.

When your Energy Type's natural strategy aligns with what BaZi says about your current Luck Pillar and what Western transits suggest is active, the convergence is particularly clear in your Cosmic Score.
