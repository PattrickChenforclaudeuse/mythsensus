How to read your score

The Cosmic Score does not say you are a "good" person or a "lucky" person. It says how loudly 26 traditions agree on the same archetype for the moment you were born. A 940 means almost every tradition reads you the same way — your archetype is easy to read at a glance. A 480 means the traditions argue with each other about you — you carry contradictions, which is a quieter kind of gift.

How are the 26 weights decided?

How granular is each system's answer? Not how popular, not how respected — how granular. Vedic Jyotish splits the sky into 27 nakshatras; BaZi into 60 stem-branch pairs; the Mayan Tzolk'in into 260 day-signs. These resolve each birth date into a narrower bucket, so their vote carries more information. Western sun signs (12 buckets) and Biorhythm (3 cycles) resolve more coarsely, so they vote with less weight. The weights are calibrated once against thousands of historical charts, then frozen.

They do not change based on who you are. A Thai reader, an Indian reader, and a Norwegian reader with the same birth date receive the same Cosmic Score. Mythsensus does not tilt toward the tradition you grew up with — your culture is not an input to your score.

How is the Cosmic Score calculated?

Each of the 26 systems is computed independently from your birth date (and where given, birth time and birth city). Each contributes a normalised sub-score; the Cosmic Score is the weighted consensus across all 26. When most agree, the score is high. When they disagree, the score reflects the disagreement rather than smoothing it away.

What input does Mythsensus need?

Birth date is required. Birth time and birth city are optional but make BaZi, Vedic and Western astrology readings significantly more accurate. No account or signup is needed to generate a Cosmic Score — the calculation runs offline in your browser.

Are the weights different for users from different cultures?

Weights are calibrated once against thousands of historical charts then frozen. A Thai reader, an Indian reader, and a Norwegian reader born on the same date receive the same Cosmic Score — your culture is not an input.
