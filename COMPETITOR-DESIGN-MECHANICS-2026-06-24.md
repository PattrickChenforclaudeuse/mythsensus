## 1. Synthesis Astrology — our CLOSEST rival (does 5-system agree/contradict consensus)
**What they built:** 5 systems (Tropical/Sidereal/Draconic/Chinese/Numerology) → one "composition" (NOT 5 separate readings — woven around agreement vs divergence).
**Onboarding:** name/email/DOB/time(opt)/location → Swiss Eph 300+ points → **free "sign composition" emailed (~60s, async not instant)** → upsell $36 one-time / $28/mo.
**Free output:** top/least-represented signs across all 5 · 300-word structural overview · **a shareable visual composition graphic** (paint-mix metaphor) · NO reading, just the map.
**Their exact framing (study this — verbatim):**
- Agreement: *"when they agree, it's worth noting"* · *"convergence across independent systems suggests something persistent"* · *"where Tropical and Sidereal agree, the pattern is structural"*
- Divergence: *"when they don't, the tension is informative"* · *"not an error — often the most psychologically interesting part"* · divergence = *"hidden complexity"* not "contradiction"
- **"Core Tension" visual** = one dominant color vs one competing color (e.g. Scorpio ↔ Gemini pulling apart).
**Paid:** $36 = 30+pg PDF + 30-day AI chat + 1 synastry · $28/mo adds 16-wk self-study course.
**❌ Their gaps = OUR opening:** only 5 systems (vs 26) · **agreement described VERBALLY, not VISUALLY** · no deity/character · no real-time consensus viz · the agreement MAP itself isn't shareable (only a static composition) · synastry costs extra.
**→ Mythsensus adapt:** steal the convergence/divergence *language* (works), but **make it VISUAL** — a 26-system consensus ring (cluster systems: Asian/Western/Elemental/Numerological, show agree/disagree spatially). Free = consensus map (% of 26 agreeing on dominant trait); paid = which systems + why + deity assignment. **Framing word: "conviction" (converge) vs "complexity" (split).**

## 2. Compatibility UX (stickiest category)
**Co-Star (friend graph):** add via contacts/username · home screen ranks "Most/Least Compatible" friends up front · 7 categories → collapse to 3 shown (intellect/emotions/love-sex) · qualitative good/bad per dim (NOT %) · B&W minimalist = screenshots feel intentional · **viral unit = the brutal daily notification, not the compat screen** (share affordance on compat screen is inconsistent = UX flaw) · negative compat as shareable as positive ("look what it said about us").
**The Pattern ("Bonds"):** 3 dims (resonance / friction / dynamics generated) · **animated pie chart** of relationship proportions · layered: read→tap deeper→listen(audio) · shares on "scary accurate" + social provocation · "Connect" = Tinder-for-astrology (paid).
**Korean Gunghap:** 2 names→% from stroke count, <60s, ultra-low friction · shared as KakaoTalk stories · % is **immediately argumentable** ("90%! see" / "40%? impossible") · cultural legitimacy = not novelty.
**→ Mythsensus adapt:** side-by-side 26-system panel for 2 people; 5 life domains (identity/emotion/communication/purpose/tension) each = "how many of 26 agree this area is harmonious vs discordant". Free = total ("19/26 agree you share emotional resonance"); paid = domain breakdown + **deity pair** (each person's deity + how archetypes interact). Zero-friction "quick compat" (2 DOB → 1 headline + card <30s) = acquisition unit. Card = 2 deity images + 1 agreement % + 1 headline; tension domain visible but not dominant (friction = share hook).

## 3. Share-card mechanics (Spotify Wrapped / Co-Star / Doubutsu Uranai)
**Spotify Wrapped (the science):** exploits **"optimal distinctiveness"** (belong AND stand out at once). Vertical card for IG/TikTok Stories ratio · bold large type (readable as screenshot) · ONE dominant color per card · **ONE key stat per card, never a dashboard** · 2025 viral card = "Listening Age" (comparative/arguable) · hook = **SPECIFICITY not breadth** ("76 plays of that one song" > "you like indie") · self-mockery is shareable · count-up number animation · Stories-style swipe (not scroll).
**Co-Star:** viral unit = one-line brutal notification, clean B&W, "personality verdict from impartial authority".
**Doubutsu Uranai:** 12 animals × 5 colors = **60 combos = the right order of magnitude** (unique-feeling but recognizable to discuss) · combo is the unit ("Black Cheetah" > "Cheetah") · card = animal art + color + 3-5 traits + 1 essence line + SNS share · **metaphor travels socially where a trait list cannot** ("I'm a wolf").
**→ Mythsensus card:** ONE deity image (dominant cross-system archetype, art >60% of card) · headline "26 systems agree: you are [Archetype]" (e.g. "The Boundary Walker") · 3 trait chips (not a list) · agreement metric "21/26 converge" · small "assigned by: Vedic+Norse+Bazi+18 more" · vertical, dark bg. **The "this is me" = inevitability via convergence: "not one system — 21 traditions say this."** Add "what's your deity?" birthdate→card→share + guess-a-friend quiz layer. Make it beautiful enough to post even without reading text. ⚠️ 1,069 deities = make abundance feel like *specificity*, not overwhelm.

## 4. Character-narrated reading (Tarotoon / HelloBot)
**Naver Tarotoon:** pick a webtoon char you already love → DOB → char delivers AI fortune **in their established voice** → funnel to read the webtoon. Works on pre-existing parasocial bond; personality filters the reading (mischievous char = mischievous reading). Chat-based. (panel structure unverified.)
**HelloBot (most detailed):** named AI characters w/ distinct personalities + **custom sticker vocabulary as emotional punctuation** · chat UI (familiar) · conversational not declarative · **reading ends with a DESIGNED collectible shareable card** (not a chat screenshot — people share designed objects far more) · collect-all gallery = return visits · Hearts currency (soft gate; 200+ free) · ⚠️ flaw: currency not explained early → surprise paywall.
**→ Mythsensus adapt (deity-as-character):** assigned deity = your reading character (visual + voice archetype + tradition-matched style; Loki ≠ Guan Yu). **Panel-delivery model (4-6 webtoon-style panels, deity in speech bubbles)** — IP-differentiating (chat is generic; panels = Mythsensus signature) + higher production value. Funnel: free = archetype reveal + 1 panel teaser → paid = full 6-panel → "explore deity's mythology" → compat = "your deity meets partner's deity" 2-char sequence. **KEY constraint: personality CONSISTENT across sessions** = system-prompted (6-8 voice archetypes: trickster/sage/warrior/lover/...), assigned at system level, NOT free-form. Each reading → collectible card.

## 5 design principles to implement
| Principle | Source | Mythsensus application |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement = conviction, divergence = complexity | Synthesis Astrology | "21/26 agree" as primary trust signal; disagreement = interesting not confusing |
| Shareable unit = ONE strong identity claim | Spotify Wrapped | deity card + archetype name + agreement count on one vertical card |
| Negative/tension is as shareable as positive | Co-Star, The Pattern | compat card shows tension clearly; friction = provocative share hook |
| Metaphor identity > trait lists | Doubutsu Uranai | deity assignment IS the metaphor ("you are Hermes" travels) |
| Character consistency = return visits | HelloBot, Tarotoon | one deity/user, same voice every session, designed card at reading end |

## 6. HOOK → STAY → PAY (payment + comment weighted, added 6-24)
**What people actually PAY for (revenue/pricing signal):** live consult = biggest $ (AstroTalk $141M · Sanctuary $20-60/sess · AstroSage) > full report/per-item (FateTell $39.99 · PDF $19-36) > **time-specific subscription** (monthly transits) > remedies/physical (AstroTalk ₹200Cr · amulets · deity cards). Compatibility = usually the paid upgrade.
**What people COMMENT/share most (engagement signal):** "too generic" (#1 complaint) · compatibility (stickiest + most-shared) · "scary accurate/feeling seen" · billing/ads anger · want-to-learn/actionable.
**HITS BOTH high-$ AND high-comment (do these first):** ⭐ **Compatibility** (paid upgrade + most-shared category) · ⭐ **Consensus depth / "not generic"** (answers #1 complaint + IS the depth tier FateTell/AstroSage monetize) · **Time-specific subscription** (recurring + ritual habit). [live/persona = most $ but heavy build · share-card = funnel feeder, not direct $]

**Retention hooks (stay longer / return):** daily 1-line verdict + push (Co-Star · Jeomsin "today" 1M/day) · **community interpretation layer** (post result → others comment; Cece/Dek-D = our biggest gap) · friend-graph/compatibility (social lock-in) · collectible cards (HelloBot gallery) · persistent memory / time-cycles.
**Conversion hooks (→ pay):** **curiosity-gap** (free teaser that resonates → reveal a verdict/depth unseen → paywall at peak curiosity; HelloBot engineers this explicitly) · "this-is-so-me FIRST, then paywall depth" (Pattern/Nebula) · quiz-onboarding sunk-cost (Nebula) · first-free + wallet (AstroTalk) · sell at high-intent moment (FateTell time-specific · New Year · "before a decision") · convert intangible→tangible (remedies/prints/cards).
**🔑 THE PATTERN:** *free = an accurate hook that proves "this is about YOU" (resonance/convergence) → builds curiosity that there's depth/a verdict you can't see → paywall right at peak curiosity.* Accuracy (free) earns belief; the curiosity-gap (paid) converts. Stickiest paid category = compatibility.
**→ Mythsensus funnel:** FREE hook = **consensus-confidence + signature card** ("21/26 systems agree you are X" = this-is-so-me + proof) → curiosity-gap = paywall *which systems / why* + deity-narrated full reading + tension detail → PAID = compatibility (cross-sys synastry) · full reading · time-specific monthly. Retention = daily consensus 1-liner + community layer + collectible deity cards.
