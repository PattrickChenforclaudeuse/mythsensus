## 🗣️ USER VOICE — real reviews (added 6-24)
2 agents mined App Store/Google Play/Reddit/Trustpilot + Pantip/Lemon8/Korean Daum cafes/Japan. This grounds the above in what users *say*, not features.

### Complaints users make A LOT (the gaps to win)
1. **"Too generic / same as everyone"** = #1 complaint in EVERY market (Co-Star, Nebula "pre-written fluff", MyHora Pantip "คำทำนายเหมือนกันทุกวันเกิด"). → **our 26-sys consensus is the literal fix.**
2. **Subscription/billing rage** — Nebula = "scam" rep (unauthorized charges + cancel traps), Co-Star "worst $50". → **radical pricing transparency + 1-tap cancel = a positioning win.**
3. **Ads** = #1 functional complaint in Korea (Jeomsin/Posteller forced countdown ads ≈50% of negative reviews). → **"no ads" is a real differentiator (we have no ad pressure).**
4. **Anxiety/doom tone** — Co-Star ("verging on a death threat"), The Pattern (relationship anxiety) harm the support-seeker segment. → **"grounded wisdom, not doom" tone.**
5. **Jargon / no "why" / not actionable** — want plain language + what to DO, and to learn.

### What users love & share (the pulls)
6. **"Scary accurate / feeling seen"** = #1 love + THE viral trigger. ⚠️ **but "accuracy"(แม่น/정확) = "resonates with me NOW", NOT predictive** — users keep using apps they call inaccurate; they re-query until it resonates. → **reframe value: "convergence confidence", not "more accurate".**
7. **Compatibility/love = stickiest category + best viral loop EVERYWHERE** (Co-Star friend-graph, Hellobot romance-tarot hook, Thai ดวงความรัก, Meemodel love-tarot).
8. **Sharing is aesthetic/emotion-driven, NOT accuracy** — Shiitake "aura colors" (100M TikTok views), Thai illustrated tarot get the most shares.

### 🎯 Biggest validation: cross-system consensus is a DOCUMENTED USER BEHAVIOR
Thai + Chinese + Korean users **already manually consult multiple systems and look for agreement** ("run the result through several interpreters to see if similar results are drawn"; re-query AI until consensus). **Users do BY HAND exactly what Mythsensus automates** = demand-side proof of the moat, not just supply-side "nobody does it".

### 💡 Demand-validated, IP-safe opportunities (beyond characters)
- ⭐ **Cross-system COMPATIBILITY (love)** — the stickiest category + the #1 viral loop + our unique structural angle (26-sys synastry, no one has it). Free love-compat = the hook. **Top pick.**
- ⭐ **"Convergence confidence" framing** — surface "20 of 26 systems agree = unusually strong signal". Answers complaint #1 + matches real user psychology (#6).
- ⭐ **Visual "Cosmic Signature" card** (Spotify-Wrapped style, aura-color per system) — shareable artifact; sharing is aesthetic-driven.
- **Thai name-science / lucky numbers** — documented HIGH unmet digital demand (specialist sites mongkolname/79hora; True telco productized lucky phone #s; ~46% of Thai spiritual spend = lucky numbers). High willingness-to-pay, low app competition.
- **"No ads + transparent pricing"** positioning — directly dodges complaints #2-#3.
- **Micro-education ("how each system sees it") + weekly warm ritual/action prompt** — answers jargon/actionable/self-reflection-therapy demand (Korea: saju = "between personality test, therapy, daily horoscope").
- **"Already decided? see what 26 systems say"** post-decision mode — serves documented post-decision validation behavior (removes accuracy pressure).

## 🛩️ WALD LENS — survivorship bias (added 6-24, director's catch)
The reviews above = the "planes that came back" (engaged survivors). The real signal is in the **silence**: people who left without reviewing + features nobody mentions. Don't armor where the holes already show — armor where they don't.

### A. Silent dislikes — why users CHURN (NOT captured in star reviews)
1. **Content exhaustion / boredom = the #1 SILENT killer.** Loud reviews = billing/ads/tone; but most churn is quiet — users "exhaust meaningful content in weeks" then just stop opening (no 1-star left behind). → **retention-over-time is the real battle and it's invisible in reviews.** A single-snapshot reading dies here.
2. **The never-converted** — the biggest "didn't come back" group = skeptics who never tried, or who bounced at the birth-form. They leave ZERO reviews. The Pattern's entire moat targets them (jargon→psychology). → our zero-friction + plain-language onboarding fights for this invisible majority.
3. **Privacy / data unease** — "apps store excessive personal data" is documented but *under-voiced* (people don't review it; they just don't enter data, or churn). → our **all-local / no-data-sent** engine is a silent-need answer **nobody markets**.
4. **"Nothing to DO with it" (not actionable)** + **notification fatigue** → quiet uninstall, not a complaint.

### B. Features NOBODY talks about
**B1 — Built-but-ignored (DEAD ZONE → don't headline):** users essentially never mention astrocartography · dream interpretation · biorhythm · facial-AI reading · "past-life portrait" · MBTI×astrology · real-time sky · palm reading · the breadth of obscure systems (Gene Keys/Kabbalah/geomancy). = novelty hooks that demo well but **don't retain.** ⚠️ **Lesson for us: keep our 21 obscure systems as consensus BACKEND fuel, NOT 21 front-facing toys.**
**B2 — Latent unserved (→ ARMOR HERE):**
- **The un-named "consensus" need** — users cross-check interpreters by hand, but no product *names or serves* it → it's absent from feature-talk **because it doesn't exist yet, not because it's unwanted** (the Wald flip). = our exact moat.
- **Trust / provenance** — "is this accurate / why should I believe it?" is felt by everyone, answered by no one → our deterministic engine + "X of 26 agree" + grounding tool.
- **Retention-over-time / an evolving reading** — the silent-churn antidote nobody ships (Nummi's memory = the only nod).
- **Privacy-as-a-feature** + **family/multi-profile management** — silent/under-served.

### C. Wald flip — where to invest
- ❌ **Don't** headline obscure-system breadth or novelty toys — survivors prove they don't retain.
- ✅ **Armor the silence:** retention-over-time · trust/provenance · the un-named consensus need · privacy — the no-hole zones that actually kill the app.
- ⚠️ **Self-check our own plan:** Signature-card + compatibility = safe (talked-about, high-engagement). But beware shipping demo-flashy features that don't retain; keep the 26-system breadth as *backend fuel for consensus*, not front-facing clutter.
