Privacy Policy

Thailand PDPA Compliant · Last updated June 2026

Mythsensus is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and international privacy standards.

Data We Collect

  • Date, time, and place of birth (used for calculation only)
  • Email address (for account and report delivery)
  • Display name or alias (optional)
  • Payment info processed securely via Gumroad (merchant of record handling card processing, tax, refunds, VAT) — Mythsensus never sees or stores card details

How We Use Your Data

  • Birth data is used solely to calculate your Cosmic Score
  • Birth data is never stored beyond report generation unless you explicitly save it
  • The 43-page Cosmic Blueprint PDF is assembled entirely in your browser — no birth data leaves your device during purchase, payment, or PDF delivery (Gumroad only sees the card transaction)
  • We never sell, rent, or share your personal data for marketing

Your PDPA Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data. Contact: privacy@mythsensus.com

Cookies

Essential cookies only for authentication and session management. No tracking cookies without consent.

Data Retention

Account data retained while your account is active. Request deletion at any time.

Age and Children's Privacy

Mythsensus is rated for general audiences. The free tier requires no account and collects no personal data — appropriate for all ages, including users under 18 supervised by a parent. For paid features (Deep Reading, Full Report, Subscription), users should be 13 or older; users aged 13–17 should obtain parental consent before purchase. We do not knowingly collect identifying data from users under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, contact privacy@mythsensus.com and we will delete it promptly. Birth dates entered for calculation are processed locally in the browser and not transmitted to our servers regardless of user age — see /how-it-works for technical details.

Social Login Considerations

If you sign in with Google, Facebook, or LINE, those providers receive a record that you authenticated with Mythsensus. For users under 18 (and for users with elevated privacy concerns), we recommend email-only sign-in instead, which does not link your Mythsensus use to any third-party identity provider. The free tier requires no sign-in at all.
