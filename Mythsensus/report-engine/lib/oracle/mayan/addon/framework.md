## What Mayan Tzolk'in measures

The Tzolk'in is the Maya 260-day sacred calendar (~2,000+ yrs continuous use).
Two interlocking cycles — 20 day-signs × 13 tones — produce 260 unique kin.
Your birth kin reveals your personal energy archetype + role in cosmic time.

| Field | Use to answer |
|---|---|
| `mayan.kin` (1-260) | Birth kin number |
| `mayan.sign` (one of 20 day-signs) | Day-sign archetype |
| `mayan.tone` (1-13) | Galactic tone — how you express the sign |
| `mayan.signTh` / `signMeaning` | Display + 1-line theme |
| `mayan.color` (Red/White/Blue/Yellow) | Direction + life-essence |
| `mayan.reading` | Engine narrative (legacy) |

## 20 day-signs × 13 tones (key references)

Day-signs (1-20): Imix · Ik' · Ak'b'al · K'an · Chikchan · Kimi · Manik' ·
Lamat · Muluk · Ok · Chuwen · Eb' · B'en · Ix · Men · K'ib' · Kab'an · Etz'nab' ·
Kawak · Ajaw.

Tones 1-13: 1-Magnetic, 2-Lunar, 3-Electric, 4-Self-existing, 5-Overtone,
6-Rhythmic, 7-Resonant, 8-Galactic, 9-Solar, 10-Planetary, 11-Spectral,
12-Crystal, 13-Cosmic.

Tones 1-4 = identify/initiate. 5-8 = stabilize/serve. 9-13 = perfect/release.

## How Mayan answers each of the 10 universal questions

- **work_energy_direction** — Cite kin + tone. Tone 1-4 → "ขึ้น"; 5-8 → "นิ่ง"; 9-13 → "แปลงร่าง".
- **work_boldest_move_window** — Months aligned to kin's galactic seal cycle. Recommend the next "Galactic Activation Portal" date if available.
- **money_flow_direction** — Yellow day-signs (K'an, Lamat, Eb', Kib', Ajaw) tend to material focus; Blue (Ak'b'al, Muluk, Ok, Ix, Kab'an) to transformation; Red (Imix, Chikchan, Manik', Chuwen, Men) to initiation.
- **money_leak_or_windfall** — When kin's reverse-mirror (260 - kin) activates → caution; harmonic partner kin → windfall window.
- **love_energy_state** — Use kin's "partner kin" + tone. Adjust per relationship_status.
- **love_timing_windows** — Kin's birthday cycle = personal new year, prime relationship momentum.
- **health_weak_point** — Each day-sign has body affinity; e.g. Imix=primal water/birth, Kimi=transformation, Kab'an=earth/spine.
- **people_who_changes_you** — Kin's antipode (130-step opposite) = the catalyst person.
- **warning_high_risk_window** — Kin's analog-shadow days within the year.
- **warning_specific** — Map by tone: tone 13 (Cosmic) → "release control"; tone 5 (Overtone) → "don't overcommit".
