## What it measures
Norse Runes (Elder Futhark, ~1,500 yrs) — 24 runic letters each a portal to a
specific cosmic energy. Birth-rune assignment by birth date period (Aett system).
Used divinatorily for guidance + life-archetype.

## Elder Futhark birth-rune themes (24 runes)
Fehu (wealth/cattle), Uruz (raw strength), Thurisaz (storm/protection),
Ansuz (Odin's wisdom), Raidho (journey), Kenaz (illumination), Gebo (gift/balance),
Wunjo (joy), Hagalaz (disruption), Nauthiz (need/constraint), Isa (stillness),
Jera (harvest), Eihwaz (yew/endurance), Perthro (mystery), Algiz (protection),
Sowilo (sun/success), Tiwaz (justice/warrior), Berkana (birth/feminine),
Ehwaz (partnership), Mannaz (humanity), Laguz (water/flow), Ingwaz (gestation),
Dagaz (breakthrough), Othala (heritage/home).

## Q→Hint
- **work_energy_direction** — Birth rune's elemental quality + aett emphasis.
- **work_boldest_move_window** — Months ruled by Raidho/Sowilo/Tiwaz energies (active runes).
- **money_flow_direction** — Fehu = primary money rune; check its presence in cast.
- **money_leak_or_windfall** — Hagalaz/Nauthiz periods.
- **love_energy_state** — Gebo + Ehwaz + Berkana. Adjust per relationship_status.
- **love_timing_windows** — Berkana (start) / Gebo (deepen) / Ehwaz (commit) / Hagalaz (release).
- **health_weak_point** — Aett's body affinity (Freyr=fertility, Heimdall=senses, Tyr=action).
- **people_who_changes_you** — Ehwaz/Mannaz archetype.
- **warning_high_risk_window** — Hagalaz transit months.
- **warning_specific** — Birth rune's shadow (e.g. Fehu shadow = greed; Uruz shadow = recklessness).

## Pricing/risks
Standard. Norse Runes birth assignment is calendar-based, deterministic.
